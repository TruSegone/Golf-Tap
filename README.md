# Golf Tap

**Golf Tap** is a bright, pick-up-and-play arcade golf game built in Java with libGDX. It turns a round of golf into a focused one-hole challenge: aim carefully, choose the right shot power, and guide the ball around course hazards before your stroke limit runs out.

## Game description

Take on a compact golf challenge where every tap, aim adjustment, and release matters. Your goal is simple: get the ball from the tee into the hole in **eight strokes or fewer**. The challenge comes from balancing direction and power. A shot that is too soft may leave the ball short, while an overpowered shot can send it past the target or into trouble.

Golf Tap is designed for short sessions and quick retries. Read the course, line up your shot, charge the power, and release at the right moment. Sand and water hazards make shot placement important, so don't just hit as hard as possible—think about where the ball will finish and set up your next stroke.

### How to play

1. **Aim your shot.** Move the mouse to adjust the direction toward the target.
2. **Charge your power.** Hold the left mouse button or the Space key to build up the shot.
3. **Take the shot.** Release the button or Space key to strike the ball.
4. **Navigate the hazards.** Avoid sand and water, and manage your shot distance.
5. **Sink the putt.** Get the ball into the hole in eight strokes or fewer.

### Objective and challenge

- Complete the hole within the eight-stroke limit.
- Balance shot direction and power rather than relying on maximum strength.
- Avoid course hazards that can disrupt your progress.
- Learn from each attempt, adjust your aim, and try to complete the hole more efficiently.

### Controls

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Aim | Move the mouse |
| Charge a shot | Hold the left mouse button or Space |
| Hit the ball | Release the left mouse button or Space |
| Restart the hole | Press `R` |
| Return to the menu | Press `Esc` |

## Play in a browser

Try the playable browser version on [GitHub Pages](https://trusegone.github.io/Golf-Tap/). The preview is deployed automatically through GitHub Pages after changes are pushed to `main`.

## Run the desktop game

On Windows, run:

```bat
gradlew.bat run
```

On macOS or Linux, run:

```bash
./gradlew run
```
